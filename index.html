What it does

Everything a family needs, and nothing it doesn't

Each feature is off until you switch it on, and each one can be switched off again just as easily.

Live location, on your terms

Sharing stays off until you start it, for a set time or until you stop. See everyone sharing with you on one map, with their last update and battery level. Stopping takes effect at once, and nobody is told that you stopped.

SOS, with no countdown

Immediate. Your family is alerted with your position and your emergency message. A private SOS PIN cancels a false alarm without anybody having to reach you first.

Safety alarm that escalates

For a walk home, a late journey, a meeting with someone you don't know. Set a countdown and choose who to contact, in order. If you don't answer, the first person is asked to check on you; if they don't respond in time, it moves to the next. The moment someone says "I've got this", everybody else is stood down.

Daily check-in

A simple "I'm fine" for the people who worry. Set a time, answer with one tap. Miss it, and the contacts you chose are told.

Chat that stays in the family

One-to-one and group chats with photos, files, voice notes and shared locations. Star what matters, reply to a single message, forward and delete.

Nearby help

Ask people near you for help, and be asked. Off until you turn it on, never shown at an exact address, and pausable any time.

Reminders that actually ring

Medicine, appointments, school runs — for you, or for someone else in your family. They ring on the phone instead of sitting unread in a list.

Your own QR code

Every person has a code. Show it or share the picture, and somebody standing next to you can add you in one scan instead of spelling out a username.

Current mode

Driving, studying, sleeping, travelling. Your family sees it next to your photo, so they know whether now is a good moment to call.

Getting started

Three steps, about five minutes

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign in with your phone number and a one-time code. No password to forget, and no social account required.

  2. 2

    Build your circle

    Invite the people who should be able to see you — scan their QR code or search their username. Each person accepts before anything is shared.

  3. 3

    Choose what to share

    Turn on live location when you want it, set a check-in time, save the places that matter. Everything stays off until you decide otherwise.

Privacy

You decide who sees you. That's the whole product.

A safety app that was careless with your data would be a contradiction. These are commitments, not settings buried three screens deep.

Your circle, and nobody else

Only the people you have added can see your location. Blocking someone hides you from them completely, and they are not told.

Never sold, never advertised against

Your data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising. SFamily carries no advertising ID and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries.

Deletion means deletion

Close your account from inside the app, or from our website if you've already uninstalled. Location history is kept for 90 days; stories disappear after about 24 hours.

Location in the background

SFamily collects location data even when the app is closed or not in use, so your family can see you on the map while you are sharing, your SOS carries where you are, and your saved places know when you come and go. You choose who sees it, every time.

Report and block, on everything

Any post, story, message, profile or help request can be reported, and you can block the person in the same step. Reports are private — the other person is never told who reported them.

Ages 13 and over

SFamily is for people aged 13 and up. Under-18s should use it with a parent or guardian who knows they have it.

SFamily is not an emergency service

It alerts the people you have chosen. It does not call the police, an ambulance, or any emergency number. In a real emergency, dial your local emergency number first.

Questions

Before you ask

Can my family see me all the time?

No. Sharing is off until you start it, and you choose who and for how long. When you stop, it stops immediately — and nobody is notified that you stopped.

Does it drain my battery?

Location only runs while you are actively sharing, and the app slows its updates when you are not moving. When sharing is off, SFamily isn't tracking anything.

What happens if I don't answer a check-in?

The contacts you chose are told, in the order you set. Each person is given time to respond before it moves to the next. As soon as one of them confirms they're handling it, everyone else is stood down.

Can I use it with friends, not just family?

Yes. "Family" is whoever you add — a partner, a flatmate, a parent, a group of friends walking home. The permissions work the same way for all of them.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's in development. Android is in closed testing now, and iOS follows. Write to us if you'd like to know when it lands.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account, inside the app. If you've already uninstalled it, you can request deletion from our website without reinstalling.
